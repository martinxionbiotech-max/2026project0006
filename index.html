Methodology & Key Metrics

Dating Pool %: estimated by multiplying independent rates: age × height × income × single rate × non-obese rate.

Matching Population: target population × Dating Pool %.

Competition Ratio: female population / eligible men in your selected pool.

Future Probability: 1 - (1 - adjusted_match_probability)years_to_40.

Note: This tool is a statistical estimator for entertainment and self-reflection, not a matchmaking guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dating reality calculator?

It estimates dating pool rarity and personal market value from demographic assumptions.

How accurate are the results?

Results are model-based estimates using static rates. They show directional realism, not exact outcomes.

Why are percentages often very low?

Combining multiple strict filters rapidly reduces the final matching probability.

Can both men and women use this calculator?

Yes. You can switch target gender and adjust criteria in the same report flow.

What does market value percentile mean?

It approximates your relative rank from weighted physical, status, and personality inputs.
